# Travel Background Image Setup

## Adding Your Travel Background Image

To complete the onboarding screen, add a beautiful travel image:

1. **Find a stunning travel photo** (recommended: 1080x1920px or higher)
   - Unsplash.com has great free travel photos
   - Search for: "travel", "adventure", "wanderlust", "airplane window", etc.

2. **Save the image as:** `travel-bg.jpg` in the `/assets` folder

3. **Recommended image sources:**
   - https://unsplash.com/s/photos/travel
   - https://unsplash.com/s/photos/airplane-view
   - https://unsplash.com/s/photos/wanderlust
